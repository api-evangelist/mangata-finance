Mangata X is about to list IMBU, the native token of the Imbue Network. Imbue is aiming to provide decentralized crowdfunding services in the Polkadot ecosystem. IMBU will be bootstrapped on the Kusama DEX with the IMBU-MGX pair on the 7th of November.
