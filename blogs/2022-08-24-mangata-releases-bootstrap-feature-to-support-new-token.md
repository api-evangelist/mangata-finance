Subtitle: Partners are ready to bring their tokens to Mangata Mangata Finance has released its new bootstrap feature. This new building block will allow any crypto project to launch a new token with deep liquidity on Polkadot and Kusama. It will enable upcoming Web3 projects to get their token into the hands of users, expose it to the market, and discover its value.
