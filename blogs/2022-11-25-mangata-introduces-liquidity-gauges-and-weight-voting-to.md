Liquidity Gauges are a powerful incentive mechanism pioneered by Curve Finance on Ethereum that has led to deep liquidity, increased governance participation, and resulted in the infamous “Flywheel Effect” and the Curve Wars. Mangata Labs is now introducing Liquidity Gauges and Weight Voting to the Polkadot Ecosystem. The first release will go live on Mangata X next week.
